Table

How the table gets set, what goes on it, and who it gets set for.

October

Eight people, one runner, and nothing that has to stay hot.

The runner was built in the morning and left alone. Everything served was room temperature or came out of one pot. The only thing that happened at seven o’clock was lighting the candles.

Still the answer

The board is the easiest way to feed eight people well.

Nothing is cooked, nothing is timed, and it can be built two hours before anyone arrives. Buy three cheeses you already like and stop deliberating.
