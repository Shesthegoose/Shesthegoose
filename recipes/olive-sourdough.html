Mediterranean Olive Sourdough

A crusty, naturally leavened loaf with briny Kalamata and buttery green olives.

Start the day before1 large loafDutch oven, 450°F then 425°F

Two kinds of olives go into this loaf: briny Kalamatas and buttery green Castelvetranos. A little garlic and lemon zest are optional. It can be made in a day, but an overnight cold proof develops more flavor, makes scoring easier, and lets you bake on your own schedule.

Ingredients

For the loaf

  • 450 g (3¾ cups) bread flour
  • 315 g (1¼ cups plus 1 Tbsp) water, divided
  • 100 g (½ cup) active sourdough starter
  • 10 g (1¾ tsp) fine sea salt
  • ½ cup pitted Kalamata olives, roughly chopped
  • ½ cup pitted Castelvetrano or mild green olives, roughly chopped
  • 1 small garlic clove, finely grated (optional)
  • 1 tsp finely grated lemon zest (optional)

Method

  1. 01

    Mix the dough. Mix the flour, starter and 300 g of the water until no dry flour remains. Cover and rest 30 minutes. Work in the salt and the remaining 15 g water.

  2. 02

    Prepare the olives. Drain the olives, pat them thoroughly dry and roughly chop. Toss with the garlic and lemon zest, if using.

  3. 03

    Stretch and fold. Complete four sets of stretch-and-folds at 30-minute intervals. During the second set, gently distribute the olives throughout the dough.

  4. 04

    Bulk ferment. Let rise until airy, slightly domed and about 40–60% larger, often 4–7 hours depending on room temperature.

  5. 05

    Shape. Pre-shape and rest 15–20 minutes. Shape into a round or oval and place seam-side up in a floured banneton or towel-lined bowl.

  6. 06

    Final proof. Overnight (recommended): cover and refrigerate for 8–16 hours after shaping, and bake directly from the refrigerator; don’t warm the dough first. Same day: cover and let rise at room temperature until noticeably puffy, usually 1–3 hours, depending on dough temperature.

  7. 07

    Bake. Preheat a Dutch oven at 450°F for 30–45 minutes. Score the dough. Bake covered at 450°F for 20 minutes, then uncovered at 425°F for 15–20 minutes, until deeply golden and 205–210°F inside.

  8. 08

    Cool. Let cool for at least 90 minutes before slicing.

Notes

Pat the olives dry before they go in. Wet olives leave wet pockets in the crumb.

If you cold proof overnight, always finish bulk fermentation and shaping before the dough goes in the refrigerator.
