Autumn Letters · No. 17

I Saw a Bear

My aunt and uncle came up from Texas this fall. It was their first time in Connecticut.

I’d been looking forward to it for weeks. When family comes to see where I live for the first time, I start making lists in my head: the places I want to take them, the things I hope they’ll notice. And since they were coming from Texas, they needed a proper New England fall.

We started with apple picking. On our way to Castle Craig, just down the road from the orchard, my aunt suddenly screamed, “There’s a bear!” She was the first to spot it, a big black bear walking across a gravel lot at the edge of the woods like it had somewhere to be.

I’ve lived here long enough to know there are bears in Connecticut. Knowing it and seeing one are two very different things. Of everything I’d planned for their visit, a bear was not on the list, and somehow it made the whole trip better.

We went on to Castle Craig, took in the view, and enjoyed the kind of fall weather that kept us outside longer than we needed to be.

I love Connecticut in the fall. The old stone walls, the winding roads, the orchards, the way the trees seem to turn overnight. But I loved it even more showing it to them.

I’ve been thinking about their visit since they left. We didn’t do anything grand. We picked apples. We saw beautiful places. We talked and laughed. We saw a bear, for heaven’s sake.

For a few days, I got to share a little piece of my life with people I’ve loved my whole life.

I suspect the bear will come up at every family gathering for years.

“This is the day which the LORD hath made; we will rejoice and be glad in it.”

Psalm 118:24 (KJV)

Until next week,
Jessica
